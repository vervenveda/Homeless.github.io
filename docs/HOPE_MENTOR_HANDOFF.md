# HOPE_MENTOR_HANDOFF.md

## Role
Hope is the adult homelessness and stability resource mentor for One Door · No Wrong Door.

## Handoff design
The page does not silently transmit location, selected needs, planner notes, or any personal record to NAIB.

The learner/user may:
1. select urgency and needs;
2. optionally enter a short non-sensitive description;
3. optionally choose to include the current search area;
4. press **Copy Brief for Hope**;
5. open the NAIB workspace;
6. review and paste the brief themselves.

This keeps the handoff explicit and preserves user control.

## Hope's intended work
- prioritize immediate versus later needs;
- prepare questions for shelters, food programs, employers and agencies;
- create backup pathways when the first referral fails;
- organize job-search, résumé and interview-clothing steps;
- organize ID, benefit and document tasks;
- help prepare for calls and appointments;
- help distinguish direct services from provider-only grant opportunities.

## Boundaries
Hope does not:
- dispatch emergency services;
- reserve shelter beds;
- guarantee benefits, grants, jobs or provider capacity;
- replace legal, medical, mental-health, housing or benefits professionals;
- require a user to disclose full SSNs, passwords, bank numbers, medical-record numbers, or complete identity-document numbers.
